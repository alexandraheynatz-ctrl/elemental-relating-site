About me

A collaborative process

Together, we will work with more than words, paying attention to the body and making room for story, context and imagination. Working with me is a collaborative process, oriented by trust, acceptance and choice.

I am an AASW Accredited Social Worker, psychotherapist, clinical supervisor and group facilitator. For more than twenty years I have worked alongside people through complexity, trauma, rupture and repair, in clinical and community settings. My training is in social work, creative arts therapy, trauma and recovery, relationship therapy and body-centred work.

I support people who want to feel more engaged and connected: to themselves, to the important people in their lives, and to their world.

Who I work with

Many of the people I work with are capable, caring and stretched thin. They give a great deal, and somewhere along the way have lost the thread back to themselves. Some are living with the effects of trauma or the cumulative impacts of stress. Some are facing questions of relationship, intimacy and sexuality. Some are ready to let go of ways of being that no longer serve them. You don't need a diagnosis to be here, just a desire to turn toward your own life.

What guides my work

Thirty years of Buddhist and mindfulness practice have shaped how I pay attention: to you, to what is said and unsaid, and to what happens between us in our sessions. I am interested in what becomes possible when we set aside what we think we know about ourselves and others, and turn toward our experience with an open mind, responsiveness and care.

  • Your body often knows before your words do.
  • We go only as fast as you can feel and trust.
  • You can learn to trust your desires and your limits, and to recognise choice.
  • You are a living system: when one part of you shifts, the whole responds.

For individuals and couples

Psychotherapy & Coaching

Here, we listen for the truth of your experience, noticing sensation, story, curiosity and calm together. We explore what softens you, what strengthens you, and what restores coherence and connection to your body, your life, your relationships.

I work with adults, as individuals and couples, at times of change, challenge and growth. This includes:

  • Relationship, sexuality and identity concerns.
  • Healing from trauma, stress and loss.
  • Navigating significant transitions.
  • Stepping into increased agency, empowerment, aliveness and connection.

Some people come for psychotherapy, to work with what is painful or unresolved. Others come for coaching, to grow into a change they are choosing. We can talk about which fits when we first speak. I meet you where you are to identify and support your path forward.

Get in touch to arrange a free 15 minute consultation. New couples referrals open in 2027.

For practitioners

Supervision & Reflective Practice

I provide supervision and reflective practice to allied health practitioners, which may include counsellors, psychologists, psychotherapists, social workers, and staff working in school wellbeing settings.

Supervision is a place to:

  • Explore alignment with different practice principles and therapeutic approaches.
  • Experiment with possibilities and edges for further professional growth and development.
  • Build sustainable ways of responding to complexity and tending to your own wellbeing.

I provide consultations for individuals and groups across a range of disciplines and service settings, including private practitioners. I have a particular interest in supporting you to notice, trust and consolidate your own deepest practice wisdom, ethics and skills.

Get in touch to talk about supervision.

In company

Groups & Workshops

I facilitate groups and workshops in three areas.

Boundaries for staying connected

Workshops focused on boundaries, choice and the agreements we make with each other, inspired by the Wheel of Consent®. Through simple, practical, embodied practices, you build skills for slowing down, noticing what genuinely feels good, and expressing your desires and limits with more ease and honesty. This is work about choice, body sovereignty and wholehearted relating.

I am a certified facilitator of the Wheel of Consent® with the School of Consent.

Fairytales for grown women

Workshops for women who want to look afresh at the stories we have absorbed about ourselves: about our bodies, about being seen, about who a woman should be. We take an old story, a fairytale or a myth, and use it to see our own lives more clearly, to set down what no longer fits, and to reclaim agency, body freedom and pleasure. There is movement, writing, embodied play and reflection, in the company of other spirited women.

Open to all women, and to gender-diverse people who recognise themselves here.

Vessel: tending grief together

Ritual-focused groups for tending grief in company. When grief is given room, so is aliveness. Grief can be for someone or something we love, for the parts of ourselves that were never welcomed, for the sorrows of the world, for what we hoped for and did not receive, and for what we carry from those who came before us. Inspired by the work of Francis Weller, Joanna Macy, Bernie Glassman and Joan Halifax.

Groups are mostly held in person. Dates for 2027 to be confirmed. Email me to register interest in future groups.

The practical things

How it works

Sessions
Psychotherapy and supervision sessions are online, for clients anywhere in Australia, and run for 50 minutes.
Fees
Fees vary by service. Please get in touch to discuss.
Rescheduling and cancelling
Individual, couple and supervision sessions can be rescheduled at no charge up to 48 hours before your session. Cancelling between 48 and 24 hours before is charged at 50% of the fee. Cancelling with less than 24 hours' notice is charged in full.
Confidentiality
What you share with me is confidential. There are some limits: where sharing information could prevent a real risk of harm to you or to another person, including a child, or where the law requires it, such as a court order. Wherever possible I will talk with you first, and I will explain these limits before we begin.
Privacy
I handle your personal and health information with care. If you would like to know how your information is collected, stored and used, please ask me.
